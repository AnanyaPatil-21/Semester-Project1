ABOUT CAMPUSPLUS

Everything You Need, In One Place

CampusPlus is a digital campus platform designed to make everyday campus activities easier for students and administrators.

Students can access important campus information, view announcements, submit complaints, and track their complaint status through a single platform.

Student & Admin Portal
